🎁 Bonus Tracker

Donations with a bonus status set in Donations_Detail. Historical Admire records may also show “Reviewed - No Bonus Recorded” when the payment was reviewed but no separate bonus assignment was preserved. Blank/unset rows are treated as not-yet-processed so nothing falls through the cracks.

📊 Campaign / Item Report

Totals for a specific fund or campaign (the "Applied To" field) within a date range. Rows with a blank Applied To are broken out separately rather than silently excluded, so the report still reconciles against the full total for the range.
